# Atari 2600 composite-video amplifier BOM

This BOM covers the components fitted to the PCB in
[`project/kicad/`](project/kicad/). Panel connectors, wire, and mounting
hardware are installation-specific and are not included.

| Reference | Value / part | Qty. |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| C1 | 47 pF ceramic capacitor | 1 |
| R1, R3 | 1 kΩ resistor | 2 |
| R2 | 3.3 kΩ resistor | 1 |
| R4 | 150 Ω resistor | 1 |
| R5 | 68 Ω resistor | 1 |
| R6 | 18 Ω resistor | 1 |
| R7 | 75 Ω resistor | 1 |
| R8 | 10 kΩ resistor | 1 |
| RV1 | 5 kΩ 3296W trimmer potentiometer | 1 |
| Q1 | 2N3904, TO-92 | 1 |
| Q2 | 2N3906, TO-92 | 1 |

For the installation, also provide RCA jacks or a composite A/V cable,
shielded wire for video, hookup wire for audio/power/ground, and a suitable
mounting method.
